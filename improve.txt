Bangladesh-Specific Improvements
Here's what matters for the Bangladeshi online seller market:
1. Eid Seasonality (highest impact)
- Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha cause massive demand spikes (clothing, gifts, food)
- Prophet can model this with yearly_seasonality=True + Eid holiday regressors
- Demand often 3-5x normal for 2-3 weeks before Eid
2. Ramadan Shift
- Night shopping increases, day-time drops
- Categories shift (food/iftar items surge, fashion dips mid-Ramadan)
3. Monsoon (June-September)
- Demand drops for many categories, spikes for rain gear, umbrellas
- Delivery delays affect reorder patterns
4. 11.11 / 12.12 / Flash Sales
- These mega-sale days create artificial demand spikes
- Can be added as binary regressors (is_sale_day)
5. Bengali New Year (Pohela Boishakh — April 14)
- Major shopping occasion, similar to Lunar New Year in other markets
What I'd Recommend Implementing (Priority Order)
1. Enable yearly seasonality — one-line change, biggest impact
2. Add product category as a feature — different categories have different seasonality (fashion vs electronics vs food)
3. Add price/promotion regressors — if the backend tracks discounts
4. Model persistence — save trained models, don't retrain per request
5. Shorter fallback for new products — drop minimum from 30 to 14 days
6. Churn model with real labels — use actual customer return patterns instead of synthetic
